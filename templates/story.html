A student-built project to make Toronto's historical subway, bus and streetcar delay data easier to explore.

OUR STORY

Why we built it

As students commuting across Toronto, we frequently experienced unpredictable transit delays. We wanted a clearer way to understand when and where those disruptions happen.

Using the City of Toronto's open data API, we built a data pipeline that collects historical TTC subway, bus and streetcar delay records and organizes them for analysis.

What the project does

The TTC Delay Analyzer brings together Python, PostgreSQL, statistical analysis, and a simple web interface. Instead of sorting through raw datasets, visitors can explore patterns and search past records by location and route, and look up the meaning of reported delay codes.
