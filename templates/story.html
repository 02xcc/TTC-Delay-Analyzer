Our Story

Why did we build the TTC Subway Delay Analyzer?

As students commuting across Toronto, we frequently experienced unpredictable transit delays. We realized that by plugging into the City of Toronto's open data API, we could build an automated pipeline to help commuters better understand subway reliability.

This project brings together data engineering, statistical analytics, and full-stack web development to turn raw CSV files into an actionable dashboard.
